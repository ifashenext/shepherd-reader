# What It Means To Become A Shepherd — transcript reader

A self-contained reader for the transcripts of the teaching series
**"What It Means To Become A Shepherd"** by **Dag Heward-Mills** (Flow Service).

- **[index.html](index.html)** — interactive reader: chapter chunking, save-your-place,
  highlights, detected scripture references, full-text search, dark mode.
- **[offline.html](offline.html)** — a zero-JavaScript version that works in any
  browser or file viewer (including iOS Files preview).

## About the text

Transcripts come from the YouTube captions of the original videos (two sessions were
transcribed from their audio). They are presented **verbatim** — paragraph and chapter
breaks are added purely for readability; no wording is changed. Scripture references are
detected from what is spoken and link back to that point in the transcript.

All teaching content belongs to **Dag Heward-Mills / Dag Heward-Mills Ministries**.
Please watch the original videos on the
[Dag Heward-Mills Camps Channel](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAoLjgFgtCwf5vP4AIxrRQJXgNvPAxMa5).
This is an unofficial personal study aid, not an official ministry publication.
